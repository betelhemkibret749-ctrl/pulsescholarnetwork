About PulseScholars Guild

PulseScholars Network is an international student-led online ecosystem built specifically for High School students (Grades 9 to 12). We remove academic burnout through collaborative Pomodoro study rooms, grade-specific peer mentorship, book clubs, and entrepreneurship talks.
